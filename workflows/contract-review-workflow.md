# CONTRACT REVIEW WORKFLOW

## STEP 1 — CONTRACT INTAKE

Identify:

- Contract type
- Counterparty
- Jurisdiction
- Business owner
- Deadline

---

## STEP 2 — PLAYBOOK MATCHING

Locate:

- Applicable playbook (ver `playbooks/master-playbook-framework.md`)
- Relevant client profile
- Relevant precedents

---

## STEP 3 — CLAUSE REVIEW

Review:

- Legal risks
- Commercial risks
- Regulatory risks
- Operational risks

---

## STEP 4 — RISK MATRIX

Classify:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

---

## STEP 5 — NEGOTIATION POSITIONS

For each material clause:

- Preferred Position
- Fallback Position
- Walk-Away Position

---

## STEP 6 — DRAFTING

Prepare:

- Redlines
- Suggested wording
- Internal comments

---

## STEP 7 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Generate:

- Top Risks
- Negotiation Strategy
- Business Impact
- Recommendations

---

## DELIVERABLES

Mandatory:

1. Executive Summary
2. Risk Matrix
3. Redline Package
4. Negotiation Positions
5. Next Steps
