# DUE DILIGENCE FRAMEWORK

## OBJECTIVE

Perform legal due diligence using a standardized methodology.

---

## WORKSTREAMS

### Corporate

Review:

- Articles
- Bylaws
- Shareholder records
- Capital structure

### Contracts

Review:

- Material contracts
- Customer agreements
- Supplier agreements
- Financing agreements

### Regulatory

Review:

- Licenses
- Permits
- Certifications
- Regulatory proceedings

### Tax

Review:

- Assessments
- Tax contingencies
- Incentives
- Audits

### Employment

Review:

- Key employees
- Litigation
- Collective bargaining

### Data Privacy

Review:

- LGPD compliance
- DPA
- Security incidents

### Intellectual Property

Review:

- Trademarks
- Patents
- Software ownership
- Licensing

### Litigation

Review:

- Judicial proceedings
- Arbitration
- Administrative investigations

---

## OUTPUT

Para cada finding, documentar:

- Issue
- Risk
- Impact
- Probability
- Recommended Action
- Responsible Party
- Timeline
- Priority
